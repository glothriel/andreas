In the little garden, by the fence, in front of a historic tenement house, a splendid rose bush catches the eye of passers-by, stately and majestic. It seductively spreads its branches, full of pink flowers that also verge slightly on orange. It preens, it flirts, and at the gentlest breath of wind it spreads an irresistible scent all around, and then it stands motionless and shamelessly bares its charms. A certain petal from a ripe flower sluggishly tears itself away from its surprised, even slightly indignant brothers and falls, resigned, onto the moss-grown soil.

Tired, with the first wrinkles on the velvet of its curves, it is almost brushing against the deadly blades of the wild grasses when a saving gust lifts it a little, and then higher and higher still. And it soars, happy, and a flirtatious little Zephyr tickles it with a gentle brush, and the petal returns the caress and seductively slips away from Zephyr, who does not give up and keeps sucking in and blowing out tender kisses, and the rosy little cloud dances, drawing circles and spirals of eastern, wanton dances in the air, and invites further frolics.

Unfortunately Zephyr notices a slender, unfamiliar twig full of white little flowers, flirtatious with their intoxicating scent. He cannot resist the temptation and flies off, full of zeal, to dance with these innocent marriageable maidens, and together with the bees he kicks off a delightful party.

The petal, again deprived of the saving breaths of air, sinks pitifully towards the greedy maw of the soil, which will soon turn it into cosmic dust; it still gazes at the sky full of piled-up clouds and feeds on the hope that for a moment longer it will dance and rejoice like a bird, swaying carefree in the embrace of the cosmos.

And then a sudden gust of air, it must be that eastern, icy gale come for the first time this year to this corner of the planet, and the petal rose high again. That moment, however, lasted too short, because the gale turned back and rushed off towards the high peaks of the Urals.

And the petal, now stripped of all hope, was still swaying in the air, and before it managed to lie down on the soil to end its life, it touched a strange, moist surface and stuck to it, spread itself out submissively and, soaked with that moisture, could no longer reach the ground.

It looked around – lots of bushes, tall grasses, tree trunks – and then it dawned on it that it had been trapped, that it had got stuck to a human arse.

Fuck – thought the petal – am I to end my life on an arse? Wouldn't it be better to lie down in the soil, at least there'd be natural fertiliser for the brother and sister plants growing here.

But the owner of that arse was of a different opinion – namely, at a certain moment something tickled him, he touched his buttock with his paw and felt the delicate membrane of something he couldn't make out what it was.

Carefully he peeled the thing off, turned his head, looked, moved the petal onto his index finger, and then suddenly a hurricane of a fart burst from his guts, at the same time everything whirled and a tornado lifted the petal into the planet's orbit.

The petal felt a piercing cold and curled up into a ball, to whose surface stardust began to stick, and in the evening sky a pink, shining sphere appeared.

Around the man, who had already managed to zip up his trousers, a crowd of onlookers gathered. People craned their necks and whispered to each other.

“Look, that must be some new star.”

“I know about these things, they haven't named it yet.”

“I propose Evening Rose.”

“And I…”

The owner of the titular arse stood nearby, cackling and repeating over and over.

“Oh fuck, what a gas, a cosmic gas.”
